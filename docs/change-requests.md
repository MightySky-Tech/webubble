# Deferred change requests

## Buyer ratings, cart and restock notifications

**Status:** Deferred; not approved for implementation in the current MVP.

The requested future experience includes verified-purchase customer reviews and ratings, sorting or recommendations based on those ratings, a transactional cart and checkout, and customer-requested restock notifications.

Before implementation, confirm the revised scope, budget and timeline. Define the verified-purchase review and moderation rules; the order, inventory and licensed payment-provider flows; and the notification provider, customer opt-in, unsubscribe and data-retention requirements. Restock messages must be limited to the customer's request and consent; unsolicited promotional messages are not included.

Until that approval and design work is complete, the storefront may show admin-approved listings and current availability only. It must not display invented ratings, promise restock alerts, or imply that products can be added to a cart or purchased. The in-scope guided product finder searches approved listings that are currently in stock and links to product details.
