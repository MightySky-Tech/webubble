# 🛡️ MIGHTSKY TECH - TRUST & CREDIBILITY STRATEGY

## Why Trust Matters

**Reality:**
- Prospects don't know you
- They've been burned before (fake promises, poor delivery)
- They're risking their money + reputation
- They need proof before committing

**Solution:**
- Build trust BEFORE the sale
- Use credibility signals at every touchpoint
- Demonstrate competence with proof
- Make promises you can deliver

## 11. COMMUNICATION BEST PRACTICES

### Response Times (Build Trust Through Reliability)

✅ **Email:** Reply within 24 hours (or same day)
✅ **Phone:** Return calls within 2 hours
✅ **WhatsApp:** Reply within 1 hour during business hours
✅ **Social Media:** Reply within 24 hours

### Meeting/Call Professionalism

✅ **Be on time** (join 5 min early)
✅ **Professional background** (clean space or blurred)
✅ **Good lighting** (you can be seen)
✅ **Clear audio** (quality microphone)
✅ **Professional attire** (shirt/top at minimum)
✅ **Take notes** (shows you care)
✅ **Confirm next steps** (follow-up email)
✅ **Send recap email** (within 2 hours)

### Contracts & Agreements

✅ **Professional contract template**
- Scope of work (clear)
- Timeline (realistic)
- Payment terms (transparent)
- IP ownership (clear)
- Support/warranty (defined)
- Confidentiality clause
- Both parties sign

✅ **NDA** (if client requests)
- Shows you're professional
- Protects both sides

## 12. TRUST-BUILDING TIMELINE

### From Cold Email to Signed Contract

```
Day 1: Cold Email
  → Specific, personal, shows research
  → Includes proof (case study link)
  → Clear CTA

Day 2-3: Follow-up (if no reply)
  → Different approach (phone/LinkedIn)
  → Reminder value

Day 4-5: Reply Expected
  → Personal response to any questions
  → Warm, not pushy

Day 5-7: Schedule Call
  → Free audit/consultation
  → 20-30 minutes
  → Show expertise during call

Day 8-10: Send Proposal
  → Professional document
  → Personalized to their needs
  → Case study included
  → Clear next steps

Day 11-14: Follow-up Call
  → Discuss proposal
  → Answer questions
  → Address concerns

Day 15-21: Negotiate Terms
  → Adjust scope/price if needed
  → Get buy-in from both sides

Day 22-25: Contract Signed
  → Professional agreement
  → Clear payment schedule
  → Project kickoff meeting
```

## 13. RED FLAGS TO AVOID (Destroy Trust)

❌ **Don't:**
- Miss email deadlines (looks unprofessional)
- Use generic email templates (shows you don't care)
- Overpromise (always under-promise, over-deliver)
- Hide pricing (clients get suspicious)
- Poor website/branding (reflects your work quality)
- No testimonials/proof (new = risky)
- Slow response times (looks disorganized)
- Typos/grammar errors (looks sloppy)
- No contract (unprofessional + risky)
- Disappear after sale (loses future business)
