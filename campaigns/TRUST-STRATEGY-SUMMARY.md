## Critical Success Factors

### ✅ DO THIS
1. **Build BEFORE selling**
   - Website → Case study → Testimonials → THEN campaign
   - Don't launch campaign until Week 2 at earliest

2. **Obsess over first impression**
   - Profile photo (professional)
   - Email (professional)
   - Website (professional)
   - These 3 determine if prospects will read further

3. **Show, don't tell**
   - "We're experienced" is a claim
   - "Here's our work" is proof
   - GitHub + case study + portfolio = proof

4. **Personalize everything**
   - Generic emails = spam folder
   - Personalized emails = meetings booked
   - Research them → customize message

5. **Respond fast**
   - Email within 24 hours
   - Phone within 2 hours
   - Chat within 1 hour
   - Speed = professionalism signal

### ❌ DON'T DO THIS
1. **Don't launch campaign before website is live**
   - Prospect searches you → finds nothing → deletes email
   - Wait for website

2. **Don't use templates without personalization**
   - "Hi [Company]..." is obvious spam
   - Write like you know them

3. **Don't undersell yourself**
   - You're not junior contractor
   - Price at market rate (~₦400-1.5M for projects, £6-20k UK)
   - Cheap = perceived as low quality

4. **Don't be silent**
   - Website with no updates = abandoned
   - LinkedIn with no posts = inactive
   - Write + share consistently

5. **Don't skip the testimonial**
   - Ask Webubble founder TODAY
   - Third-party endorsement > self-praise
   - Most powerful trust signal

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Launching campaign before website is live
**Why it fails:** Prospect googles you → finds nothing → assumes scam
**Cost:** 5-10x higher CAC (customer acquisition cost)
**Solution:** Wait for website

### ❌ Mistake 2: Generic "spray and pray" emails
**Why it fails:** Looks like spam, gets deleted
**Cost:** 70-80% lower reply rate
**Solution:** Personalize every email (takes 5 min per email, worth it)

### ❌ Mistake 3: Underselling yourself
**Why it fails:** Low price = perceived as low quality
**Cost:** Wrong type of clients, constant negotiations
**Solution:** Price at market rate, justify with ROI

### ❌ Mistake 4: Slow response times
**Why it fails:** Shows you're disorganized
**Cost:** Prospects go to competitors
**Solution:** Respond within 24 hours, always

### ❌ Mistake 5: No testimonials
