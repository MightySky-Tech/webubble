## 📧 Cold Email Formula

**Subject:** Personalized, specific
**Opening:** Reference something specific about THEM
**Problem:** Their problem (not your solution)
**Proof:** Similar client result
**CTA:** Clear, low-pressure
**Signature:** Professional, complete contact info

**Length:** 50-75 words
**Time:** 5-10 minutes per email
**Reply rate target:** 10-15%

## 📱 Social Proof Hierarchy

**Level 1: Written Testimonial**
- Text quote from client
- Format: "MightySky Tech was great..."
- Power: Medium

**Level 2: Named Testimonial**
- Text + client name + company + title
- Format: "— John Doe, CEO, Acme Corp"
- Power: Strong

**Level 3: Photo Testimonial**
- Text + name + photo + company
- Format: Includes headshot
- Power: Very strong

**Level 4: Video Testimonial**
- Client on video talking about you
- 30-60 seconds
- Power: Strongest

**Start with Level 2 (Webubble). Add Level 3-4 as you go.**
