## 💡 THE CORE PRINCIPLE

Every document is built on this foundation:

```
TRUST = Credibility × Consistency × Delivery

Credibility:   Professional presence (website, profiles, proof)
Consistency:   Same messaging everywhere, reliable communication
Delivery:      Do what you promise, on time, on budget

Build all three → Clients trust you → Sales happen
```

Skip any one, and trust breaks.

## 🚫 DON'T START HERE (Common Mistakes)

❌ **DON'T:** Jump to email campaign before Week 2
- Prospect searches you → finds nothing → deletes email
- **DO:** Build website first (Week 1-2)

❌ **DON'T:** Use generic email templates
- Generic = spam folder
- **DO:** Personalize every email (5 min per email)

❌ **DON'T:** Launch campaign before case study is complete
- Prospect asks "Show me examples" → you have nothing
- **DO:** Have case study + testimonial ready before campaign

❌ **DON'T:** Underprice your work
- Cheap = perceived as low quality
- **DO:** Price at market rate (₦400-1.5M Nigeria, £6-20k UK)

❌ **DON'T:** Delay asking for testimonials
- Ask at project completion (not 6 months later)
- **DO:** Ask for testimonial in Week 1 of project completion
